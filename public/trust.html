Trust & safety

Safety is a product feature.

Ennvo is built for real people, which means safety cannot be an afterthought. Here is how the platform approaches community standards, reporting, and enforcement.

Ennvo is an independent social platform founded by Erfan Sarker and co-developed by Rita Akter. No platform can review every moment in real time, so Ennvo combines clear rules, user reporting, and technical controls. This page explains how those pieces fit together.

Our approach

Safety work is never finished. Our approach is to set clear expectations, make reporting easy, review reports fairly, and remove accounts or content that create a serious risk. We aim to be transparent about what we can and cannot do.

Community standards

  • Respect people. No harassment, threats, hate, or targeted abuse.
  • Be authentic. Do not impersonate another person or organization to mislead people.
  • Protect privacy. Do not share someone else's private information without permission.
  • Do not post harmful or illegal content. This includes child sexual exploitation, terrorism, incitement to violence, and illegal goods.
  • No spam or manipulation. Do not use fake accounts, bulk messages, or deceptive links.

The full explanation is in the community guidelines guide. These standards apply to profiles, posts, reels, stories, comments, messages, and calls.

Safety tools

  • Block: stop an account from interacting with you.
  • Report: send content or an account to the Ennvo team for review.
  • Privacy controls: choose who can see your content and contact you where the feature is available.
  • Account security: use a strong password and never share verification codes.

How reporting works

Use the report option on the account, post, comment, story, or message. Choose the reason that best matches the problem and include a short description. For issues that need a human review, you can also open a support ticket.

Reports are reviewed by the Ennvo team. Not every report leads to removal, because context matters. A report is a request for review, not a guarantee of a particular outcome.

Enforcement

Depending on the issue, we may remove content, issue a warning, limit an account, suspend it, or ban it. Serious cases may be referred to the appropriate authorities where required by law. We aim to apply these rules consistently and to explain the reason for significant actions where possible.

Appeals

If you believe a decision was wrong, contact support with the account name and the content involved, and ask for a review. Keep the request factual. Do not create new accounts to get around a suspension.

Urgent safety

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. Ennvo is not an emergency service and cannot dispatch help. After contacting the authorities, you can report the issue on the platform so the team can review the account or content.

Parents and guardians

Ennvo is not intended for children under 13. Parents and guardians should talk with young people about what they share, who they talk to, and how to use block and report tools. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact support.

Security research

If you find a security issue, contact ennvoapp@gmail.com with a clear description and steps to reproduce it. Do not access other people's accounts, do not test on accounts you do not own, and do not publish details before the issue can be reviewed. We appreciate responsible disclosure.

Contact

For safety questions, use the Support Center and choose Safety report, or email ennvoapp@gmail.com. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for the legal framework.
