What we will never ask for

Ennvo support will never ask for your password, a one-time verification code, or payment to recover your account. If someone claims to be from Ennvo and asks for those things, do not reply. Report the message through the app or send the details to ennvoapp@gmail.com.

Response times

We aim to review every message. Response times depend on the number of requests and the urgency of the issue. Safety reports and account access problems are prioritized. Please include your device, browser, and a clear description so the team can help faster.

Start with a ticket.

The Support Center prepares a ticket with an ID and opens your email app with the details ready to send. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself.
