Getting started

Create your account and profile.

Open ennvo.vercel.app in a modern browser. Choose the sign-up option and follow the prompts. If you already have an account, sign in with the option you used before.

After signing in, add a profile photo, your name, and a short bio. A complete profile makes it easier for friends to recognize you and helps people find the right account.

Ennvo works in the browser. For quick access, use your browser's install or add-to-home-screen option if it is available. On Android, you can also use the Ennvo app.

Set up your profile in four steps

  1. Open your profile or account settings.
  2. Add a clear profile photo.
  3. Write a short bio that tells people who you are.
  4. Review your privacy settings before posting.

Reels & stories

Share short videos and everyday moments.

To create a reel, open the create option in Ennvo, record a new video or upload one from your device, trim the clip, add any available effects, write a caption, and publish. The exact editing tools can change as the app is updated.

Stories are for short-lived updates. Use them for quick moments, behind-the-scenes posts, or a simple update without creating a permanent post. Stories disappear after 24 hours.

Only upload content you have the right to share. Ask for permission before filming other people or using someone else's music or video.

For detailed walkthroughs, read the Ennvo reels guide and the Ennvo Stories guide.

Messages & calls

Chat and call from one place.

Open your messages to find your conversations. Tap a conversation to read and send messages. To start an audio or video call, open the conversation and use the call button.

The first time you make a call, your browser may ask for microphone and camera access. Allow access if you want the call to work. If you change your mind later, update the permissions in your browser or device settings.

If a call does not connect

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Allow microphone and camera permissions for Ennvo.
  • Close other apps that may be using the camera or microphone.
  • Refresh the page and try the call again.
  • Try a different browser if the problem continues.

For more, read the Ennvo video calling guide.

Account & privacy

Keep your account under your control.

Update your name, photo, bio, and other profile details from your profile or account settings. Save your changes before leaving the screen.

Use a strong password and never share it with anyone. Ennvo will not ask you for your password in a public comment or an unexpected message. If you receive a suspicious request, do not reply with personal information.

Review your privacy settings and choose who can see your posts and contact you. Be careful about sharing your location, phone number, or other sensitive details in public posts. Read the Privacy Policy for the full picture.

To request account deletion, look for the option in Settings where available. If you cannot find it, contact support from the email address linked to your account.

Safety

Block, report, and protect yourself.

If an account or post makes you uncomfortable, use the block or report option in the account or content menu. Blocking stops that account from interacting with you. Reports are reviewed by the Ennvo team.

Never send money, gift cards, passwords, or verification codes to someone you met online. Be careful with links that ask you to sign in from an unexpected message. Open Ennvo directly instead of using a link from a stranger.

If you believe someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. For platform safety issues, use the Trust & Safety page or open a support ticket.

Troubleshooting

Try these steps first.

  • Check your internet connection and try again.
  • Refresh the page or close and reopen the app.
  • Use an up-to-date version of Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox.
  • Clear the browser cache if the page is stuck on an old version.
  • Allow microphone and camera permissions for calls.
  • Try a smaller video file if an upload keeps failing.

If the problem continues, note the device, browser, and exact error message. That information makes it much easier for support to investigate.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Ennvo.

Search above or filter by topic to narrow the list.

What is Ennvo?

Ennvo is an independent social platform founded by Erfan Sarker and co-developed by Rita Akter. You can watch and share reels, post stories, chat, and make HD audio and video calls.

How do I create an Ennvo account?

Open ennvo.vercel.app and choose the sign-up option. Follow the prompts, then add a profile photo and a short bio.

Is Ennvo free to use?

Yes. Ennvo is free to use. Your internet provider may charge for data when you watch videos or make calls.

How do I create a reel on Ennvo?

Open Ennvo, choose the create option, record or upload a short video, trim it, add any available effects, write a caption, and publish.

How do I post a story on Ennvo?

Use the story option in the app, capture or choose a photo or short video, add any available text or effects, and share it. Stories disappear after 24 hours.

How do I make an audio or video call on Ennvo?

Open a conversation with the person you want to call, then use the audio or video call button. Allow microphone and camera access when prompted.

Why is my microphone or camera not working?

Check that the browser or app has microphone and camera permission for Ennvo. Close other apps that may be using the camera, refresh the page, and try again.

How do I update my Ennvo profile?

Open your profile or account settings, edit your name, photo, and bio, then save your changes.

How do I reset my password?

Use the password reset option on the Ennvo sign-in screen if it is available for your account. If you cannot reset it, contact support and do not share your password or verification codes.

How do I block or report someone?

Open the account or content menu and choose block or report. Reports are reviewed by the Ennvo team. Blocking stops that account from interacting with you.

How do I delete my Ennvo account?

Look for account deletion in Settings where it is available. If you cannot find it, contact support from the email address linked to your account and request deletion.

Who can see my Ennvo content?

Visibility depends on your privacy settings and the type of content. Review your profile and privacy controls, and remember that stories and public posts can be seen by a wider audience.

Why is Ennvo not loading or working properly?

Check your internet connection, refresh the page, try a modern browser, and clear the browser cache if needed. If the problem continues, contact support with your device and browser details.

Which browsers does Ennvo support?

Ennvo works best in current versions of Chrome, Edge, Safari, and Firefox on mobile and desktop.

How do I contact Ennvo support?

Open a ticket at ennvo.vercel.app/support or email ennvoapp@gmail.com. Include your device, browser, and a short description of the problem.

Does Ennvo work offline?

Ennvo needs an internet connection for feeds, messages, uploads, and calls. Some previously loaded app assets may remain available, but live features require a connection.

How is my data handled?

Read the Ennvo Privacy Policy for details about the information collected, how it is used, and the controls available to you.

How do I report a security issue?

Contact ennvoapp@gmail.com with a clear description and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not include other people's private data. Do not test an issue on accounts you do not own.
