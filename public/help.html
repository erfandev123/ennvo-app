Getting started

Create your account and profile.

Open ennvo.vercel.app in a modern browser. Choose the sign-up option and follow the prompts. If you already have an account, sign in with the option you used before.

After signing in, add a profile photo, your name, and a short bio. A complete profile makes it easier for friends to recognize you and helps people find the right account.

Ennvo works in the browser. For quick access, you can use your browser's install or add-to-home-screen option if it is available.

Reels & stories

Share short videos and everyday moments.

To create a reel, open the create option in Ennvo, record a new video or upload one from your device, trim the clip, add any available effects, write a caption, and publish. The exact editing tools can change as the app is updated.

Stories are for short-lived updates. Use them for quick moments, behind-the-scenes posts, or a simple update without creating a permanent post.

Only upload content you have the right to share. Ask for permission before filming other people or using someone else's music or video.

For a detailed walkthrough, read the Ennvo reels guide.

Messages & calls

Chat and call from one place.

Open your messages to find your conversations. Tap a conversation to read and send messages. To start an audio or video call, open the conversation and use the call button.

The first time you make a call, your browser may ask for microphone and camera access. Allow access if you want the call to work. If you change your mind later, update the permissions in your browser or device settings.

For call troubleshooting, read the Ennvo video calling guide.

Account & privacy

Keep your account under your control.

Update your name, photo, bio, and other profile details from your profile or account settings. Save your changes before leaving the screen.

Use a strong password and never share it with anyone. Ennvo will not ask you for your password in a public comment or an unexpected message. If you receive a suspicious request, do not reply with personal information.

Review your privacy settings and choose who can see your posts and contact you. Be careful about sharing your location, phone number, or other sensitive details in public posts.

Troubleshooting

Try these steps first.

  • Check your internet connection and try again.
  • Refresh the page or close and reopen the app.
  • Use an up-to-date version of Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox.
  • Clear the browser cache if the page is stuck on an old version.
  • Allow microphone and camera permissions for calls.
  • Try a smaller video file if an upload keeps failing.

If the problem continues, note the device, browser, and exact error message. That information makes it much easier for support to investigate.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Ennvo.

What is Ennvo?

Ennvo is the official social media app by Erfan Sarker and co-developer Rita Akter. You can watch and share reels, post stories, chat, and make HD audio and video calls.

How do I create an Ennvo account?

Open https://ennvo.vercel.app/ and choose the sign-up option. Follow the prompts to create your account, then add a profile photo and bio.

Is Ennvo free to use?

Yes. Ennvo is free to use. Your internet provider may charge for data when you watch videos or make calls.

How do I create a reel on Ennvo?

Open Ennvo, go to the create option, record or upload a short video, trim it, add any available effects, write a caption, and publish.

How do I make an audio or video call on Ennvo?

Open a conversation with the person you want to call, then use the audio or video call button. Allow microphone and camera access when prompted.

How do I update my Ennvo profile?

Open your profile or account settings, edit your name, photo, and bio, then save your changes.

Who is behind Ennvo?

Ennvo was founded by Erfan Sarker, a software engineer from Bangladesh, with co-developer and partner Rita Akter. Learn more on the About page.

Why is Ennvo not loading or working properly?

Check your internet connection, refresh the page, try a modern browser, and clear the browser cache if needed. If the problem continues, contact Ennvo support with details about your device and the error.

How do I contact Ennvo support?

Use the Customer Care & Help Center in the app's Settings. You can also email support@ennvo.com with details about the problem.

Still stuck?

Contact Ennvo support.

Use the Customer Care & Help Center in the app's Settings for account-specific help. You can also email support@ennvo.com.

Include your device, browser, a short description of the problem, and any error message. Do not send your password or verification codes.
