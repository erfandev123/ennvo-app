What is Ennvo?

A faster way to stay close to the people who matter.

Ennvo is an independent social platform for sharing short video reels, posting stories, sending instant messages, and making real-time HD audio and video calls. It is designed to be fast, simple, and easy to use on mobile and the web.

Whether you want to discover new creators, share a moment with friends, or start a conversation, Ennvo brings social discovery and real-time communication together in one place.

Ennvo is free to join. Open the app, create your account, and start connecting with friends and communities.

Our mission

Make social connection feel fast, simple, and human.

FAQ

Questions about Ennvo.

What is Ennvo?

Ennvo is an independent social platform for sharing short video reels, posting stories, chatting with friends, and making HD audio and video calls.

Who founded Ennvo?

Ennvo was founded by Erfan Sarker, who is the founder, CEO and owner of the platform. He leads a team of 10 people, working closely with partner Rita Akter.

Who is Rita Akter?

Rita Akter is a partner and core team member at Ennvo, working alongside founder and CEO Erfan Sarker on the platform's development.

Who is on the Ennvo team?

Ennvo is built by a team of 10 people led by founder, CEO and owner Erfan Sarker. The team includes Rita Akter (partner & core team), Evan Sarker (developer), Joi Akter and Junaid Mia, plus the rest of the engineering, design, support and safety members.

Is Ennvo free to use?

Yes. You can create an Ennvo account and use the core social features for free.

Does Ennvo support video calls?

Yes. Ennvo supports real-time HD audio and video calls, along with instant messaging and stories.
