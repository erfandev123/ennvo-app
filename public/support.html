Support FAQ

Before you send.

Does this form send my message automatically?

No. The form creates a ticket ID and prepares an email in your own email app. Nothing reaches Ennvo until you press Send in that app.

What should I include in a support ticket?

Include the email linked to your Ennvo account, the feature involved, what you expected, what actually happened, your device and browser, and any error message. Do not include passwords or verification codes.

How do I report a safety issue?

Choose Safety report in the form and include the account or content involved. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. You can also read the Trust & Safety page.

How do I make a privacy or data request?

Choose Privacy / data request in the form. Use the email address linked to your account so the team can verify the request. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
