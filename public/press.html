Boilerplate

Ennvo is an independent social platform founded in 2026 by Erfan Sarker and co-developed by Rita Akter. Built in Bangladesh, Ennvo brings short video reels, stories, instant messaging, and HD audio and video calls into one fast, simple experience. The platform is free to join and available on the web and Android.

Ennvo is a product-focused platform company. Its public website is the official source for company information, product guides, support, and trust pages.

Company facts

  • Name: Ennvo
  • Also known as: Ennvo App, Ennvo Social
  • Founded: 2026
  • Founder: Erfan Sarker — Founder & Software Engineer
  • Co-developer & partner: Rita Akter
  • Location: Bijoynagar, Brahmanbaria, Bangladesh
  • Product: Social platform with reels, stories, messaging, and HD audio/video calls
  • Platforms: Web and Android
  • Price: Free to use; standard data charges may apply
  • Official website: ennvo.vercel.app

Brand usage

  • Use the name “Ennvo” exactly, with a capital E.
  • Do not imply that Ennvo endorses a product, service, or person without written permission.
  • Do not use the Ennvo name or logo for a fake account, phishing page, or misleading promotion.
  • Link to ennvo.vercel.app when citing the company.
