Ennvo ("Ennvo", "we", "us") operates the Ennvo social platform at ennvo.vercel.app and related applications. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, shared, and protected when you use Ennvo.

By using Ennvo, you acknowledge that your information will be handled as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the platform.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account information: your name, email address, telephone number if you provide one, profile photo, bio, and sign-in identifiers.
  • Content: posts, reels, stories, comments, messages, profile information, and other material you upload or send.
  • Support information: your name, email, ticket details, and any attachments or information you choose to include in a support request.
  • Reports and safety information: information you submit when you report content, an account, or a safety concern.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and technical information: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, language, and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Usage information: pages viewed, features used, interactions, timestamps, crash reports, and diagnostic logs.
  • Local storage and cookies: essential information used to keep you signed in, remember preferences, cache app assets, and support features such as the support ticket form.

Information from other sources

If you sign in through a third-party authentication provider, we may receive basic account information from that provider, such as your name, email address, and profile image, subject to your settings with that provider.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and maintain your account, authenticate you, and provide the platform;
  • deliver content, messages, notifications, audio calls, and video calls;
  • personalize your experience and help you discover people and content;
  • keep Ennvo safe, investigate abuse, and enforce our Terms and community guidelines;
  • respond to support requests, reports, and privacy requests;
  • monitor performance, diagnose problems, and improve the product;
  • comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests;
  • communicate important service, security, or policy updates.

3. Legal bases

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on one or more of the following: performance of a contract with you, our legitimate interests in operating and improving Ennvo, your consent, and compliance with legal obligations. You can withdraw consent where we rely on it, without affecting processing that already happened.

4. When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers that help us host, operate, secure, and support Ennvo, including hosting and content delivery, authentication, database, storage, messaging, and diagnostic providers. These providers are expected to protect information and use it only for the services they provide.
  • Other users when you choose to share content or communicate with them. Remember that public posts, reels, stories, and profile information can be seen by a wider audience.
  • Legal and safety authorities when required by law or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.
  • A successor entity in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate protections.

We may also share aggregated or de-identified information that does not reasonably identify you.

5. Calls and messages

Messages and call data are transmitted so they can be delivered to the intended recipients. We do not record the content of your audio or video calls by default. We may process call metadata, such as the participants, time, duration, and technical status, to deliver the call, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot problems.

Other participants may record or screenshot a call or message. Do not share sensitive information with someone you do not trust.

6. Cookies and local storage

Ennvo uses cookies and browser storage for essential functionality, such as keeping you signed in, remembering preferences, caching app resources, and supporting the support ticket form. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but blocking essential storage may prevent parts of Ennvo from working.

If we introduce additional categories of cookies in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent.

7. How long we keep information

We keep information for as long as needed to provide Ennvo, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Account information is generally kept while your account is active. When you request deletion, we will delete or anonymize information within a reasonable period, subject to legal, security, and backup requirements. Some information may remain in backups for a limited time.

8. Security

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, including encryption in transit and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Use a strong password, keep it private, and contact us if you believe your account has been compromised.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information; to object to or restrict certain processing; to withdraw consent; and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. To make a request, contact ennvoapp@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

10. Children

Ennvo is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we will take steps to delete it. Parents and guardians can contact support to raise a concern.

11. International transfers

Ennvo may use service providers located in different countries. As a result, your information may be processed outside your country of residence. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Ennvo changes. When we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Continued use of Ennvo after an update means you accept the revised policy.

13. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to ennvoapp@gmail.com. You can also use the Support Center or the Contact page.
