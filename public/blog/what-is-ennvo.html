What is Ennvo? The official social media app explained

Ennvo is a free social media app for sharing short video reels, posting stories, chatting with friends, and making real-time HD audio and video calls. It is built by founder Erfan Sarker and co-developer Rita Akter.

A social app built around real connection

Ennvo brings the features people expect from a modern social platform into one simple experience. You can watch and share short videos, post a story about your day, send a message, or start a live conversation. The goal is to make everyday sharing feel fast and uncomplicated.

Ennvo is the official app from the Ennvo team. The public website at ennvo.vercel.app is the main entry point, and the About page explains who is behind the project.

What can you do on Ennvo?

  • Watch and share reels: discover short videos and publish your own.
  • Post stories: share moments that disappear after a while.
  • Send messages: keep up with friends through instant chat.
  • Make HD audio and video calls: talk in real time from a conversation.
  • Discover people and content: find creators and trending posts.

Who builds Ennvo?

Ennvo was founded by Erfan Sarker, a software engineer from Bangladesh. He leads development with Rita Akter, who is a co-developer and partner at Ennvo. You can read more about both of them on the About Ennvo page.

The platform is built with a focus on speed, simple social sharing, and real-time communication. Because the team is small, feedback from users can directly shape what gets improved next.

Is Ennvo free to use?

Yes. Ennvo is free to use. You can open the website, create an account, and start exploring. Standard data charges from your internet provider may still apply when you watch videos or make calls.

How to get started on Ennvo

  1. Open ennvo.vercel.app in a modern browser.
  2. Create an account or sign in with an available sign-in option.
  3. Add a profile photo and a short bio so friends can recognize you.
  4. Explore the feed, follow people you know, and watch a few reels.
  5. Share your first post, story, or reel when you are ready.

Why public pages matter

Most of Ennvo is designed for signed-in users, which keeps the social experience personal. The public pages — including this blog, the Help Center, and the About page — explain the product and help people find the app through search engines and AI assistants.
