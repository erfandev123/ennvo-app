How to create and share reels on Ennvo

Reels are short videos for sharing a moment, a skill, an idea, or a quick update. This guide walks through the basic flow on Ennvo, from opening the create screen to publishing your video.

What is a reel?

A reel is a short, vertical video that plays in the Ennvo feed. Reels are designed to be quick to watch and easy to share. You can record something new or upload a video you have already made.

Before you start

  • Make sure you are signed in to your Ennvo account.
  • Check your internet connection before uploading a video.
  • Record in a well-lit place and keep the important action in frame.
  • Have permission to use any music, video, or image that is not yours.

Create a reel step by step

  1. Open Ennvo and go to the create option in the app navigation.
  2. Choose the reel or short-video option, then record a new video or select one from your device.
  3. Trim the clip to the part you want to share. Keep the opening seconds clear so viewers know what the video is about.
  4. Add any available finishing touches, such as music, effects, or text. Use them only when they make the video easier to enjoy.
  5. Write a short caption that explains or adds context to the video. Add relevant hashtags if they genuinely describe the content.
  6. Preview the reel, choose the audience option you want, and publish it.

The exact labels and editing options can change as Ennvo is updated. If a button looks different, look for the same action in the create screen or visit the Help Center.

Tips for a better reel

  • Start with a hook: show the most interesting moment early.
  • Keep it short: a focused clip is easier to watch to the end.
  • Use good light: natural light or a bright room makes a big difference.
  • Keep the camera steady: rest your phone or hold it with both hands.
  • Write a useful caption: tell viewers what they are watching.
  • Be consistent: regular posts help people recognize your style.

Share responsibly

Only post videos you have the right to share. Do not upload someone else's private information or content that violates community rules. If you are filming other people, ask for their permission before publishing.

If a reel does not upload, check your connection, try a smaller file, and refresh the page. The Help Center has a troubleshooting checklist for common problems.
