Create your Ennvo account the right way

A good first setup makes Ennvo easier to use later. Here is a careful, step-by-step way to create your account, build a clear profile, and start with the right privacy settings.

Before you start

You need a modern browser and an internet connection. Ennvo works on the web, and an Android app is also available. Use a device you control, especially if you plan to stay signed in.

Have a profile photo ready. A clear photo of your face or a simple image you are comfortable representing you is enough. You can change it later.

Create your account

  1. Open ennvo.vercel.app.
  2. Choose the sign-up option.
  3. Follow the prompts and provide the requested details.
  4. Complete any verification step shown on screen.
  5. Sign in if you are not signed in automatically.

If you already have an account, use the sign-in option instead of creating a second one. Multiple accounts can make it harder to manage your content and privacy.

Set up your profile

A complete profile helps people recognize you and reduces the chance of confusion with an impersonator. Open your profile settings and add:

  • A profile photo that is clear and belongs to you.
  • Your name as you want friends to see it.
  • A short bio that says something useful about you.
  • Any available links you are comfortable sharing publicly.

Avoid putting your home address, phone number, or identity document numbers in your public bio. A bio is visible to more people than a private message.

Review privacy before you post

Before sharing your first post, open the privacy settings and decide who can see your content and contact you. The best setting depends on how you plan to use Ennvo. If you are unsure, start more private and open up later.

Read the Privacy Policy for the full explanation of what Ennvo collects and how it is used.

Find people and content

Use search to find people you know and topics you care about. Follow a few accounts, watch a few reels, and explore the feed. Your experience improves as you interact with the content you actually want to see.

Be careful with follow requests from accounts you do not recognize. You do not have to accept every request, and you can block an account at any time.

Set up notifications

Notifications help you keep up with messages, comments, and follows. If you enable them, choose the categories you actually want. If notifications become distracting, turn off the ones you do not need instead of disabling everything.

On mobile browsers, notification permission is controlled by your browser and device settings. If Ennvo notifications stop appearing, check those settings first.

Secure your account

  • Use a strong password that you do not reuse on other sites.
  • Never share your password or verification codes.
  • Sign out on shared devices.
  • Review your active sessions if the option is available.
  • Be suspicious of messages that create urgency or ask for money.

If you think someone accessed your account, change your password immediately and contact support. Explain what happened and include the account details that are safe to share. Never send your password to support.

Common setup problems

I did not receive a verification message

Check your spam or junk folder, confirm that you entered the correct address or number, and wait a little before trying again. Do not request a new code repeatedly; it can make the process slower.

My profile photo will not upload

Try a smaller image in JPG, PNG, or WebP format. Check your connection and refresh the page. If the problem continues, contact support with the file type and size.

I cannot sign in

Use the password reset option on the sign-in screen if it is available. If you still cannot get in, contact support from the email address linked to your account. Do not create a new account to report a login problem unless support asks you to.

If you want to leave

If you no longer want to use Ennvo, look for account deletion in Settings where it is available. If you cannot find it, contact support from the email address linked to your account and request deletion. Deleting your account is different from simply signing out; signing out leaves the account in place.

The short version

Create one account, complete your profile, review privacy before posting, and protect your sign-in details. A few minutes of setup now prevents most problems later.
