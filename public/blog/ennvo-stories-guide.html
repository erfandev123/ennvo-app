How to use Stories on Ennvo: a simple guide

Stories are the quickest way to share a moment on Ennvo. This guide explains how they work, how to post one, and how to keep your updates comfortable and safe.

What is a story on Ennvo?

A story is a short photo or video update that disappears after 24 hours. Unlike a permanent post or reel, a story is designed for something temporary: a view from your day, a quick announcement, a behind-the-scenes moment, or a thought you do not want to keep on your profile forever.

Because stories are time-limited, they are a low-pressure way to stay active. You can share something small without worrying that it will sit on your profile for months.

How to post a story

  1. Open Ennvo and sign in to your account.
  2. Choose the story or create option in the app.
  3. Take a new photo or video, or choose one from your device.
  4. Add any available text, effects, or stickers.
  5. Review who can see the story, then share it.

The exact buttons can change as Ennvo is updated, but the flow stays the same: capture or select media, add a finishing touch, and publish. If the story option is not visible, make sure you are signed in and using an up-to-date version of the app or browser.

How long does a story stay visible?

Stories are intended to disappear after 24 hours. Once that window passes, the story is no longer shown in the normal story row. This is different from a reel or profile post, which stays available until you delete it.

Do not use a story for information you need to keep permanently. If something should be easy to find later, publish it as a post or a reel instead.

Who can see your story?

Story visibility depends on your account and privacy settings. Before posting, review the audience option shown on the story screen. If you are not sure who can see it, treat the story as public and avoid including private details.

Think carefully before sharing your location, phone number, workplace, or anything that identifies where you are at a specific time. Even a short-lived story can be screenshotted by someone who sees it.

Stories versus reels

Stories and reels serve different purposes. A story is temporary and informal. A reel is a short video that stays on the platform and can be discovered by more people. If you want a quick update, use a story. If you want to build an audience around a video, use a reel.

You can read the Ennvo reels guide for a full walkthrough of recording, trimming, captioning, and publishing short videos.

Story ideas that feel natural

  • A quick look at your workspace, study desk, or daily routine.
  • A photo from a walk, a meal, or a moment with friends (with their permission).
  • A short update about something you are working on.
  • A question or poll for the people who follow you.
  • A behind-the-scenes clip that does not need a permanent post.

Keep your stories safe

Share only content you have the right to post. Ask before filming other people, and do not upload someone else's photo or video without permission. If a story makes you uncomfortable, use the block or report option in the app. The privacy and safety guide explains those controls in more detail.

If you make a mistake, delete the story as soon as you notice. Deleting it removes it from the normal story view, though someone may already have taken a screenshot.

Common story problems

The story button is missing

Check that you are signed in, refresh the page, and make sure you are using a current browser or app version. If the problem continues, contact support with your device and browser details.

A story will not upload

Check your internet connection and try a smaller photo or video. If the upload still fails, close and reopen Ennvo and try again.

You cannot see someone's story

The story may have expired, the person may have deleted it, or their privacy settings may not include you. Respect their choices and do not ask other people to share private content.

The short version

Stories are for temporary, everyday updates. Post thoughtfully, review your audience, avoid sensitive details, and let the moment disappear when its time is up. For permanent content, use a post or a reel.
