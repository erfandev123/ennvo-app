Ennvo community guidelines, explained

Community guidelines are not fine print. They are the shared rules that keep a social platform usable. Here is what Ennvo expects from people who use the platform, in plain language.

Why guidelines exist

A social platform works only when people feel safe enough to participate. Guidelines set a baseline: respect people, do not harm them, do not deceive them, and do not use the platform to break the law. They apply to posts, reels, stories, comments, messages, profiles, and calls.

The goal is not to police every opinion. The goal is to stop behavior that makes other people unsafe or unwilling to take part.

Respect people

Do not harass, threaten, or target someone with repeated unwanted contact. Do not publish someone's private information to intimidate or expose them. Do not create content that encourages violence or celebrates someone else's suffering.

Disagreement is allowed. Abuse is not. If a conversation turns hostile, step away, block the account, and report serious behavior instead of escalating it.

Be authentic

Do not impersonate another person or an organization in a way that could mislead people. Do not use fake accounts to manipulate conversations, evade a block, or inflate engagement. If you represent a business or project, make that clear.

Parody and commentary can be legitimate, but they should not be confused with the real person or brand. Do not use someone else's name, photo, or logo to deceive people.

Protect privacy

Share only content you have the right to share. Ask before posting a photo or video of another person, especially in a private setting. Do not post private messages, identity documents, financial details, or someone's address without permission.

If you are a parent or guardian, talk with young people about what they share and who they talk to. Ennvo is not intended for children under 13.

Do not post harmful or illegal content

Ennvo does not allow content that sexually exploits children, promotes terrorism, incites violence, sells illegal goods, or violates another person's intellectual property. Do not use the platform to coordinate harm or to evade a legal restriction.

If you see content that may involve imminent danger, contact local emergency services first, then report it on the platform.

No spam or manipulation

Do not send bulk unsolicited messages, run deceptive promotions, or use automated accounts to distort activity. Do not post the same content repeatedly in unrelated conversations. Do not use misleading links or fake login pages.

If you want to promote a business or project, be transparent and follow the same rules as everyone else. Attention earned honestly is more durable than attention taken by force.

How reporting works

Use the report option on the account, post, comment, story, or message. Include the reason that best matches the problem. If you are reporting a serious safety issue, contact support as well and include the account name and a short description.

Reports are reviewed by the Ennvo team. Not every report results in removal, because context matters. A report is a request for review, not a guarantee of a particular outcome.

What happens after a report

Depending on the issue, the team may remove content, issue a warning, limit an account, suspend it, or ban it. Serious cases may be referred to the appropriate authorities where required by law.

Enforcement is applied as consistently as possible, but the team may make mistakes. If you believe a decision was wrong, contact support with the details and ask for a review. Do not create new accounts to get around a suspension.

Appeals and questions

If your content was removed or your account was limited, you can ask for an explanation. Use the Support Center and include the account name and the content involved. Keep the request factual and concise.

For more detail about safety tools, read the Trust & Safety page and the privacy and safety guide.

The short version

Respect people, be honest about who you are, protect private information, do not post harmful or illegal content, and report problems instead of escalating them. These rules keep Ennvo usable for everyone.
