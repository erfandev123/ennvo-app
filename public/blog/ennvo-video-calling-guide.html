Ennvo video calling guide: clear HD audio and video calls

Ennvo supports real-time HD audio and video calls, so you can talk to friends and family without leaving the app. This guide covers the basic call flow and the most common fixes when something does not work.

Before you start a call

  • Connect to a stable Wi-Fi or mobile data network.
  • Allow Ennvo to use your microphone and camera when your browser asks.
  • Use headphones if you are in a noisy place.
  • Check that your device is not in silent mode if you expect the call to ring.

How to make a call on Ennvo

  1. Open Ennvo and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to your messages or open a conversation with the person you want to call.
  3. Look for the audio or video call button in the conversation.
  4. Choose an audio call or a video call.
  5. Allow microphone and camera access if this is your first call.

The exact placement of the call buttons may vary slightly as the app is updated. If you cannot find them, open the conversation menu or visit the Help Center.

During the call

Use the in-call controls to mute your microphone, turn your camera on or off, switch audio output, or end the call. If the other person cannot hear you, check that you are not muted and that the correct microphone is selected.

Common call problems and fixes

No sound

  • Unmute the call and check your device volume.
  • Try headphones or a different speaker.
  • Close other apps that may be using the microphone.

Camera is not working

  • Check your browser's camera permission for Ennvo.
  • Close other apps that may be using the camera.
  • Reload the page and try the call again.

The call keeps breaking up

  • Move closer to your Wi-Fi router or switch to a better network.
  • Ask the other person to check their connection too.
  • Try an audio-only call if the video stream is unstable.

Call privacy

Treat calls like any private conversation. Do not share passwords, verification codes, or financial details with someone you do not trust. Be careful when taking calls on public networks, and check your device's screen and microphone permissions if you are using a shared computer.

Still need help?

If a call still does not work, note what happened, the device and browser you used, and any error message you saw. Then contact support from the app or use the Ennvo Help Center for more troubleshooting steps.
