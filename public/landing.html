Ennvo is not just an app — it is an official social media platform operated by a dedicated team. Founder Erfan Sarker leads engineering, and co-developer Rita Akter is a partner in development. Together they are building Ennvo to be fast, reliable, and accessible to anyone with a browser or a phone.

What Ennvo includes

The full platform, A to Z.

From short videos and stories to private messages and live calls — here is what you can do on Ennvo.

Short video reels

Watch, create, and share short-form videos in the feed.

Stories

Share everyday moments that disappear after 24 hours.

Real-time messaging

Instant chat with friends and media sharing.

HD audio calls

Clear, low-latency voice calls.

HD video calls

Face-to-face real-time video conversations.

Creator discovery

Find new creators, communities, and trending content.

User profiles

Personalize your profile with photo, name, and bio.

Privacy controls

Choose who can message you and see your content.

Notifications

Alerts for likes, comments, follows, and mentions.
