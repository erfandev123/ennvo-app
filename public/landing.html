The company

A social company built around people, not algorithms.

Ennvo is an independent social platform founded in 2026 in Bangladesh. Our goal is simple: make everyday connection feel fast, clear, and human again. Instead of filling the screen with noise, Ennvo focuses on the moments people actually came for â€” a reel worth watching, a story worth sharing, a message worth sending, or a call worth making.

Founder Erfan Sarker leads product and engineering. Rita Akter is a co-developer and partner, working across the platform's development. Together they lead Ennvo's product and engineering direction, with a focus on shipping carefully and listening to users.

Ennvo is free to join and available on the web and Android. The public website is the official home for company information, product guides, support, and trust pages.

How we build

Four principles behind every release.

Fast by default

Pages and feeds should feel instant on real networks, not just fast Wi-Fi.

Private by design

Clear controls, sensible defaults, and no surprises about who can reach you.

Simple by intention

Every screen should make the next action obvious. Less noise, more connection.

Built for everyone

A social platform should work for a first-time smartphone user and a power user alike.

Questions, answered

Ennvo at a glance.

What is Ennvo?

Ennvo is an independent social platform founded by Erfan Sarker and co-developed by Rita Akter. You can share reels, post stories, message friends, and make HD audio and video calls.

Who owns Ennvo?

Ennvo is built and operated by its founding team. Erfan Sarker is the founder and software engineer; Rita Akter is a co-developer and partner.

Is Ennvo free?

Yes. Ennvo is free to join and use. Standard data charges from your internet provider may apply when you watch videos or make calls.

Where can I get help?

Visit the Help Center for guides and answers, or open a support ticket for account-specific help.

Does Ennvo work on mobile?

Ennvo works in modern mobile browsers and is also available as an Android app. You can add the web app to your home screen for quick access.

How is my data handled?

Read the Privacy Policy for the full explanation of what we collect, how it is used, and the controls available to you.
